# Kaervax OS — Operating Model

## Operating domains

| Domain | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Product | Product readiness and customer-facing experience |
| Distribution | Channel presence, listings, traffic and conversion |
| Partnerships | Sellers, creators, affiliates and resellers |
| Commerce Ops | Orders, fulfillment coordination, returns and exceptions |
| Growth | Campaigns, content, experiments and acquisition |
| Intelligence | KPI, demand signals and decision support |
| Automation | Repetitive workflow execution |
| Governance | Access, audit, policy and change control |

## Decision hierarchy

1. Customer demand
2. Transaction evidence
3. Operational reliability
4. Unit economics
5. Repeatability
6. Scale opportunity

A new build should have a documented reason connected to at least one of these signals.

## Operating cadence

### Daily
- order/lead exceptions
- channel health
- urgent customer issues
- fulfillment blockers

### Weekly
- channel performance
- product performance
- creator/affiliate activity
- campaign results
- operational bottlenecks
- next experiments

### Monthly
- channel expansion review
- unit economics
- partner quality
- automation candidates
- roadmap reprioritization

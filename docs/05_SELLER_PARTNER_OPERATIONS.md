# Kaervax OS — Seller & Partner Operations

## Partner types

- reseller
- marketplace seller
- distributor
- referral partner
- wholesale partner
- operational partner

## Partner lifecycle

```
Prospect → Qualified → Onboarding → Active → Reviewed → Paused/Exited
```

## Partner record

Minimum fields:

- partner_id
- type
- contact reference
- channel
- territory (if applicable)
- approved products
- commercial terms reference
- status
- owner
- last activity
- notes
- compliance/terms status

## Onboarding

1. confirm identity/business context
2. explain product and terms
3. define approved distribution method
4. provide catalog/assets
5. establish attribution method
6. confirm fulfillment/support responsibilities
7. activate
8. review first activity

## Governance

Commercial terms must be explicit and versioned. Do not rely on undocumented verbal assumptions for price, commission, returns, or brand usage.
